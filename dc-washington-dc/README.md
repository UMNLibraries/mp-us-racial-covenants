# District of Columbia racial covenants

## Suggested citation

*Cherkasky, Mara; Shoenfeld, Sarah; Corey, Michael; Petersen, Penny; Gillette, Rebecca; Delegard, Kirsten; Rogers, Kelly; Palacios, Amelia; Mattke, Ryan; crowdsourcing community mapmakers. (2025). U.S. Racial Covenants Series in Washington, D.C., Hosted by Mapping Prejudice.*

## Data notes

Racial covenants in Washington DC in this dataset represent a combined representation of several phases of work with differing methodologies. While the column structure in the data files is the same for Washington DC as in the other jurisdictions in the dataset, not all values are filled in, and there are some differences.

- Note on dates: In the earlier phases of DC work, only the year and not the exact date of the covenanted document were transcribed. Therefore, the `deed_date` field in DC covenants collected in early phases is January 1 of the year of the restriction, rather than the precise date.

- DC researchers were not able to research property records from 1916-1921. As of 2026, digitized records before 1921 are not available.
